Why Pre-paid Metering in Hostel/ PG Accommodations
AT HOSTEL & PG ACCOMMODATIONS, managing electricity isn’t easy.
Uneven usage across guests, wasteful usage, misuse, or disputes over
accuracy of manual analog meters readings can lead to mis-trust,
confusion, and extra work for site managers/ owners.
These issues are best tackled through a Smart Pre-paid metering solution.
BIJLEE BUDDY is an intelligent, customizable and future ready IoT
enabled pre-paid electric metering solution that empowers PG managers
with centralized remote control to leave all the electricity management
related worries behind; and build a transparent & harmonious environment
for guests.
4

How it Works

BIS approved electricity meters enabled with smart metering solution are deployed for every room

They are connected through control wiring to a Gateway Device; Optional RF connectivity available

Additional devices installed for metering and monitoring DG specific activities - separate reading, separate rates and separate
load management - if opted; Additional meters can be deployed for common area and/ or overall reference metering

Gateway Device connects, through mobile 4G networks, to our Cloud Servers
where all metering data is captured and processed

Monitoring, Controls, Payments and Data Reports are enabled for
Hostel/ PG managers and Guests through web dashboards and mobile apps
