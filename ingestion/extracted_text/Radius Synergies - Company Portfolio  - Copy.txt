30,00,00,000 / 96 ≈31,25,000 meters

Each meter sends 48 blocks/day
So, the number of meters supported =
30,00,00,000 / 48 ≈62,50,000 meters
Approx. 62.5 Lakh [ 6.2 Millions ] smart meters (with 30-min interval profile data)
