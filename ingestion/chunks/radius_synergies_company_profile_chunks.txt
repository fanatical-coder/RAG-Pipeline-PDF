Company Profile Company Details Attribute Information Company Name Radius Synergies International Private Limited Company Type Private Limited Company Incorporation 6 August 2008 Authorized Capital I2 Crore Paid-up Capital I1.78 Crore Headquarters Dwarka, New Delhi, India Company Overview Radius Synergies International Pvt. Ltd. is an Indian technology company specializing in energy management, smart metering, Internet of Things (IoT), and automation solutions. The company focuses on developing intelligent systems that integrate hardware, software, and analytics to help organizations monitor and manage energy usage more efficiently. Mission To deliver innovative, scalable, and sustainable solutions that empower businesses and communities with seamless connectivity, energy management, and automation technologies. Vision To build a technology-driven ecosystem where intelligent infrastructure improves efficiency, sustainability, and quality of life. Core Technology Areas • Internet of Things (IoT) • Machine-to-Machine (M2M) Communication • Smart Energy Management Systems • Smart Metering Infrastructure • Data Analytics and Automation Platforms Products and Solutions Smart Prepaid Metering Systems, Energy Monitoring Platforms, Integrated Building Management Systems (IBMS), and Smart Grid Solutions that allow real-time monitoring and optimization of energy consumption. Industries Served Power utilities and DISCOMs, real estate and smart buildings, industrial infrastructure, smart city projects, and energy utilities. Leadership
